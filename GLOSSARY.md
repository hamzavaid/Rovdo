# Glossary

- **BYOK:** Bring your own key: use a user-selected model provider with credentials stored in the OS vault. Local-first storage does not imply local inference.
- **Collection scope:** Broad boundaries that determine which postings enter the collected inventory, distinct from filters applied when displaying results.
- **Eligibility:** PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN for supported job constraints, separate from a qualification score.
- **Employer directory:** A catalog of employers and their career boards, distinct from the inventory of collected job postings.
- **Evidence:** Source references and excerpts supporting a candidate fact or qualification.
- **Job collection:** Gathering job postings from selected sources within the collection scope, independently of candidate qualification matching and result filters.
- **Opportunity fit:** A separate ranking method that can include location, salary and employer preferences.
- **Result filtering:** Narrowing collected job postings for display without removing them from the collected inventory.
- **Starter employer directory:** The employer directory bundled with the app to provide initial collection coverage without requiring users to add companies.
- **Technical score:** A deterministic 0–100 indicator derived from job requirements and verified supported qualifications; not a hiring probability.
- **Verification:** Explicit user confirmation of a supported fact; import or AI extraction alone does not verify it.
